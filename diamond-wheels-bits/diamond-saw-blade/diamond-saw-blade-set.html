Instructions for use

1. Pretend to pay attention to the coordination and rotation direction of the center hole of the motor shaft and the saw blade

2. Do not cut the metal, otherwise it will be a sharp -cutting of the saw blade and shorten life

3. Please take a few minutes before cutting. After confirming that there is no swing or beating, use it normally

4. In order to ensure the safety of the use, the cloud stone machine or angle grinding machine of the saw blade must be covered with a protective cover

5. The protection device should be available when wearing, please do not wear loose clothes during work
