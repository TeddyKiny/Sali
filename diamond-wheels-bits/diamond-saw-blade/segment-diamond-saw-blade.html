SALI brand 4.5 inch (115mm) turbo diamond saw blades’ detail information as below:

SIZE: Each diamond saw blade is 4.5" in diameter.

DESIGN: The turbo style rim combined with an even diamond distribution make these blades ideal for cutting a wide variety of materials. The cooling holes provide extended life by reducing core heat.

APPLICATION: These diamond saw blades are primarily designed for Marble, Granite, Stone,and

also be used on Concrete, Brick, Block, Stone, Masonry Materials and more.

USE: Our turbo diamond saw blades can be used for wet and dry cutting. If you are concerned with dust generation, we suggest wet cutting.
