For sawing wood, particle board, and medium-density boards, left and right teeth should be selected, which can cut the wood fiber structure sharply and make the incision smooth; in order to keep the groove bottom flat and grooved, use flat teeth or use left and right flat combined teeth; sawing Ladder flat teeth are generally used for veneer and fireproof boards. Because of the large cutting rate of computer cutting saws, the alloy saw blades used are relatively large in diameter and thickness. The diameter is about 350-450mm, and the thickness is between 4.0-4.8mm. Most Use ladder flat teeth to reduce chipping and saw marks. The rake/clearance angle is 15 degrees.


Welding method

A: High-frequency welding (manual, semi-automatic and fully automatic) Skin effect and adjacent effect produced by high-frequency current; this welding is characterized by high efficiency, fast speed, good repeatability, fast and uniform heating, and effective prevention of saw blades Embrittlement and fracture caused by local overheating can also prevent tooth loss caused by unwelded.

B: Alloy welding: Alloy welding is biased towards low-temperature welding to protect the base and alloy from damage during welding. The welding temperature is not higher than 75 degrees Celsius.


Resistance welding is generally used in the market, which is not as stable and firm as high-temperature welding, and the appearance after welding is different. The appearance of high-frequency welding is more beautiful, the welding temperature is more stable, and the quality of the alloy bit is not easily affected.
