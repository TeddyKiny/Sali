SALI MIG WELDING MACHINE MIG-200S

【EASY TO USE】: The welding machine is designed as a single-knob intelligent control system, coordinating the speed and current of the wire feeder.
The precise digital display guides your welding work and provides a perfect weld.

【RELIABLE PERFORMANCE】: This MIG gasless welder machine will control voltage automatically and current to achieve a smooth arc by synergy function, ideal for welding beginners.

【SAFE AND STABLE】:The built-in overheating and overcurrent protection system and the fan cooling system will keep dust and dirt out, thus extending the machine's life.

【PORTABLE DESIGN】: The wire feed welder weighs only 4.5kg and the split torch is easy to set up and transport, allowing you to move around.

【PURCHASE WITH 100% CONFIDENCE】:What you get: 1*2M Gas-Shielded Welding Gun, 1*1.5M Earth Clamp, 1*Protective Mask, 1*Gasless Flux Cored Wire (1.0kg-0.8/1.0mm), 1* Instruction manual.
