SALI TIRE PRESSURE GAUGE 1/4"
High-precision mechanical pointer tire pressure gauge
Chinese factory, support OEM, ODM


WHAT YOU CAN GET:

This tire inflator gauge includes an aluminum tire inflator and deflator, a large dial mechanical air pressure gauge, a 13" long maximum pressure up to 250PSI rubber hose, and a brass straight chuck with a leak-free valve core.

It is a functional tool ready for testing tire pressure and deflating and inflating tires with an air pump or compressor.

PREMIUM TIRE INFLATOR:

The SALI AUTO tire inflator gauge has a surface-frosted aluminum handle.

An equipped brass air bleeder button deflates the tire whether the tire inflator is connected to an air pump.

You can also press the chrome-plated iron thumb trigger to inflate the tire when the 1/4" FNPT connector is connected to an air pump or compressor.

ACCURATE GUARANTEE TIRE PRESSURE GAUGE:

The tire pressure gauge uses a superior aluminum dial plate, passes the specified test and is calibrated to international accuracy standards.

The large dial is protected by a gear-style rubber protector, is perfect for truck bus RV car UTV ATV motorcycle and bike (with Schrader valve). No battery is needed and leak-free.

WHY YOU NEED AN AIR INFLATOR GAGE:

A reasonably inflated tire saves fuel, increases the tire's lifespan, improves the driving experience, and ensures driving safety.

An air inflator deflator gauge can reduce the tools and streamline the processes when inflating.

Please make sure to use a tire pressure gauge to test the wheel pressure every week regularly when the tire is cold, especially before long-distance travel.
