1. product Introduction

The 30L model is suitable for air nail gun, wind batch, woodworking decoration, car washing and dust blowing, not for spray painting.

Oil free mute 50L and above can be used for painting. If it is used for a long time uninterrupted spray painting work, it is recommended that customers take high power and large capacity to work. In addition, the motor heating does not exceed 85 degrees are in line with the standard. Some customers are afraid to use the motor as soon as it heats up, which is totally unnecessary. We are pure copper motor, normal operation, proper maintenance will not burn machine.

2. Product advantage

(1) Wide range of applicable pressure, piston compressor can be designed into low pressure, medium pressure, high pressure and super high pressure, and at the same speed, when the exhaust pressure fluctuation, the displacement of piston compressor basically remains unchanged.

(2) High compression efficiency, piston compressor compressed gas process is a closed system, its compression efficiency is high.

(3) strong adaptability, piston compressor displacement range is wide, and the impact of gas density on the performance of the compressor is not as significant as the speed compressor piston compressor of the same specification can be applied to the compression of other gas medium as long as a little transformation.Application
