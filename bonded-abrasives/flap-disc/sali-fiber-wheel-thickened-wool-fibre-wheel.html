Technical Parameters

Fiber is a kind of polishing material, also known as fiber polishing wheel, nylon fiber, polypropylene fiber, cutting force is strong, can be partly replacing traditional cloth wheel, wheel and rubber sponge cloth emery cloth wheel, covering cloth belt left by the thick line, larger burrs of fine except for casting and even after the excellent effect on repairing and grinding grain fine and beautiful. Can be used for manual and automatic grinding, middle or rear line treatment and to eliminate less visible surface imperfections

In high speed polishing machine (3000 RPM) on the use of no burn workpiece surface, no blackening, strong cutting force, sharp, durable, efficient, blocking, high smoothness and good effect. Fast heat dissipation, high speed grinding resistance, no residual glue and black silk on the polished workpiece, high wear resistance, high elasticity, strong self-sharpening, good brightness of the workpiece, small dust, no pollution, safety and noise.

It is made of nylon fiber with high toughness and high temperature resistance, selected abrasives and special adhesive. Fiber wheel is a multi-layer structure using the "side" of the mesh surface as the cutting surface of the rotation direction is not restricted, easy to use, its open mesh structure can resist high temperature, reduce the parts and grinding wheel contact discoloration and deformation risk, make grinding wheel more wear-resistant. The product is used to remove metal burrs, fine polishing, coarse drawing, line line harmonization, improve the surface condition of the workpiece without obviously changing the shape and size of the workpiece, in the grinding process of fresh abrasive constantly acting on the machined surface, to provide a stable and uniform polishing effect.

Fiber wheel can throw plane, concave and convex surface and all kinds of grooves, the surface of the thrown metal workpiece is matte, mercerized, until the mirror, the best use state for coarse and fine casting. Aircraft, automobile and other parts processing, various metal workpiece surface treatment, stainless steel tableware, building hardware, handle, hinge, door lock, electroplating, lighting, golf clubs, copper and aluminum products, bronze bronze electroplating sweep pattern, wood products, plastic, marble polishing, etc.

1. Polish the metal surface, remove the edges, and gently wire draw the surface of the lock after electroplating. Cast stainless steel, alloy, copper, aluminum, iron and other hardware workpiece, sand grain effect is good, straight lines, good wear resistance.

2, ceramic, marble, polishing brick and other stone industry with water, surface polishing and mirror polishing.

3, non-metallic surface polishing, casting round corners, such as furniture, wood crafts, resin crafts, etc
