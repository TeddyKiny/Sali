Technical Parameters

SALI Diamond Concrete Grinding Wheels

Feature：

1.With a diameter of 4.5 inches (115mm), 5/8-11 threaded interface, 27 diamond segments, and 30/40 diamond grit. The maximum speed reaches 12200 RPM, suitable for both wet and dry use, and compatible with angle grinders.

 

2.Adopting special-shaped and angled diamond particles to optimize grinding and removal efficiency. The unique design not only improves work efficiency but also effectively reduces noise during operation, providing a more comfortable working experience.

 

 

3.Optimized for grinding and coating removal, suitable for various materials such as concrete, asphalt, epoxy coating, tile glue, urethane, and paint. Achieves high-precision surface treatment with fine grinding and removal results.

 

4.Made with high-strength steel body to ensure stable cutting performance and excellent surface finishing. Heat-treated steel body with large grinding segments enhances durability and wheel life, reducing long-term usage costs.

5.Using molded sintering and single-layer brazing technology to create solid diamond tools. Molded diamond segments demonstrate excellent performance with significantly extended tool life, ensuring long-term stable work performance.
