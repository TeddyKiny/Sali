SALI UNIVERSAL PU FOAM
High - Performance And Versatile Gap - Filling Product For Your Hardware Import&export Business

I. Core Product Advantages

(1) Outstanding Performance

• Dense Closed - Cell Structure: Boasts a fine - textured surface that remains in excellent condition throughout the year, ensuring an aesthetically pleasing gap - filling result.

• High - Strength Sealing: Effectively prevents water infiltration, addressing the waterproofing needs of gaps in doors, windows, pipes, and more, eliminating the risk of water leakage.

• Ultra - High Bonding Strength: Perfectly conforms to gaps and adheres tightly, guaranteeing a long - lasting sealing effect.

• Multi - Functional Use: Combines thermal insulation, acoustic insulation, and shrinkage resistance properties. It meets customers' diverse requirements for gap treatment, such as enhancing indoor sound insulation and maintaining a stable temperature.

• Fast - Setting: Can be trimmed 1 hour after application, saving construction time and improving work efficiency.

(2) Wide Applicability

• Temperature Adaptability: Applicable for construction at temperatures ranging from +5°C to +50°C, with an optimal range of +18°C to +35°C. After curing, it can withstand temperatures from - 30°C to +80°C, making it suitable for projects in various climatic regions.

• Material Compatibility: Bonds well with materials like concrete, masonry, metal, wood, and plastic (except Teflon and silicone), covering a wide range of hardware construction scenarios and adaptatiovarious building and decoration materials.
 

(3) Convenient and Practical

• Dual - Use System: Features a gun/straw dual - use system, offering flexible operation to meet different construction habits and scene requirements, even for beginners.

• Packaging Specification: Comes in a 750ml large - capacity can, allowing for more gap - filling per can and reducing customers' procurement frequency and costs.

II. Rich Applicable Scenarios

Whether it's for filling gaps in attics and interiors, sealing pipes and tanks, constructing roofs, facades, and foundations, or working on basements, floors, partitions, as well as car bodies, trailers, boats, and yachts, this PU foam excels. It caters to the diverse needs of overseas projects in the hardware export business.
