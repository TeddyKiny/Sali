Products Description


SALI high quality Glass Suction Cups 2 heads and 3 heads

High Quality: The suction cups for glass is made of durable body,electric polishing and natural odorless rubber base that can withstand work site abuse.

Load Capacity: Two Claws the maximum horizontal suction of this heavy duty suction cup is 120kg and the maximum vertical suction is 150kg. Three Claws the maximum horizontal suction of this heavy duty suction cup is 160kg and the maximum vertical suction is 200kg. The powerful suction lifter make materials handling easier and Safer.

Ergonomic Design: With quick release and locking lever for each window suction cup,the black flip-down tabs makes it convenient to lock the suction in place and provides stability. The handle has a striped design for effective non-slip,perfect for long time use.

Wide Application:  Our double suction cups can be an ideal large glass lifter especially for lifting,moving,pulling,gripping,handling materials such as glass windshield window tiles mirror and granite.

Attention: Please use the auto glass suction cup on a CLEAN, SMOOTH, FLAT and CURVED surface.
