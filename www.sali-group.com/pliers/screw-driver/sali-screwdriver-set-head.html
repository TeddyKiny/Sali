Technical Parameters

Screwdriver head, usually a screwdriver head mounted on a hand drill or hammer. An electric screwdriver is a small electric tool used to tighten or loosen screws.

Batch head according to different head type can be divided into a word, cross, rice word, star, square head, hexagonal head, Y head and so on, among which a word and cross is the most commonly used in life.

Hex head is not used much, commonly used hex wrench, like a lot of screws on some machines with hex holes, convenient multi-angle force.
