Technical Parameters

Production Introduction

HIGH QUALITY MATERIAL: The pvc pipe cutter use Stainless Steel blade coated with Teflon has rust prevention, hardness up to HRC55 and low cutting firction. 

ADVANCED STRUCTURAL DESIGN: With a TRIANGLE blade, the SALI PVC Cutter penetrates the pipe wall with less presure which presents less damage to the pipe, and less harm to hand. With the excellent ratchet mechanism, the user can complete the cutting operation with one hand quickly, both opening and closing. The ergonomic rubber covered handle is and labor-saving, and will not hurt your hand when working continuously.

HUMANIZED DETAILS: the bottom of the handle is equipped with a safety latch, which makes the storage more convenient and safe. The blade can be replaced simply and quickly without special tools.

WIDE APPLICATION RANGE: SALI pipe cutter can cut many kinds pipe up to 2-1/2".O.D, such as PPVC, CPVC, PP-R, PEX, PE, rubber pipe, etc. As for the cutting pipe diameter, the standard size of the pipe cutter is 2-1/2inch, which refers to the outer diameter of the pipe. Compared with the standard pipe size in the market, this product is suitable for cutting pipes with the outer diameter of 2-1/2inch.
