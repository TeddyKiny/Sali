Description

-The durable and reliable Folding Utility Knife from SALI features an aluminum handle, with a folding and locking mechanism that reveals an all-purpose cutting blade for easy one-handed operation. 


-The quick blade exchange system accepts standard utility blades, and a comfortable thumb cushion is located at the top of the oversized handle, so you can apply pressure and leverage when cutting into thick material. 

-The handle opens to a storage compartment, which includes five utility blades, and there's also a stainless-steel belt clip to keep the knife at your waist.
