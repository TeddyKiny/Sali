HOW TO MAINTAIN PLIERS?
1. Cleaning: You need to clean it after using a pliers. You can wipe the surface of the pliers with a dry cloth, or you can wipe it with a cleaner, but be careful not to use too strong cleaning agents.

2. Rust -proof: Dinger is easy to rust, so apply a layer of anti -rust oil or engine oil before and after use to protect the pliers from being influenced by humidity and oxidation.

3. Reduce impact: avoid excessive power and avoid impact when using pliers, otherwise it will affect the accuracy of the pliers and damage the pliers.

4. Storage: After use, the pliers are stored in dry and ventilated places to avoid moisture oxidation.

5. Maintenance: Long -term use of pliers should be maintained regularly, such as replacing aging spring, fastening screws, etc. to extend the service life.
