This 7 inch cutting wheel, 180x1.6x22.2mm is normal size and hot-selling in Yemen, Ethiopia, Oman, Saudi Arabia.

Except the Aluminum oxide , we also add some white aluminum oxide, all the composition is mixture but write down the main percents grit number in the label.

The appearance of white corundum is white, the hardness is slightly higher than that of brown corundum, but its toughness is slightly worse. It is easy to cut into the workpiece during grinding. It has good self-sharpening, less heat generation, strong grinding ability, high efficiency, and the price is higher than brown corundum.

It is suitable for grinding steel with high hardness, such as stainless steel, high speed steel, high carbon steel, hardened steel, alloy steel, etc.

The particles of single crystal corundum abrasives are composed of single crystals and have good multi-edge cutting edges. Compared with A and WA, they have higher hardness and toughness, strong grinding ability, and less grinding heat. The disadvantage is that the production cost is high, waste gas and waste water are generated in the production, and the output is low.
