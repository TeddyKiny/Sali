SALI BENCH GRINDING WHEEL

GRINDING WHEEL for Double-Wheeled Bench Grinders for comprehensive grinding on several materials

A Bench Grinding Wheel is a grinding wheel composed of abrasive grains bonded together in a matrix to form a circular shape.
It is used in various grinding applications such as removing rust, sharpening and finishing materials.
It is made from high-quality corundum, making it a durable and reliable tool for any type of grinding or sharpening project.
It can be used for both wet and dry applications, making it a versatile product.
The wheel is designed to fit most standard bench grinders, making it a great option for any workshop.

SPECS:6" x 1" x 1-1/4",6" x 3/4" x 1-1/4",8" x  1"x 1-1/4", comes with 1", 3/4” , 5/8" ,1/2" telescoping bushings.

MATERIAL:Designed with quality white aluminum oxide (WA),high hardness, good wear resistance.

IDEAL GRINDING TOOL: It has high brittleness, sharp abrasive grain and good thermal conductivity.

APPLICATIONS: Suitable for grinding of hard and heat sensitive steel, such as hardened steel, high carbon steel,alloy steel and so on.

USER EXPERIENCE: During the grinding process, the grinding heat generated by the sharpening wheel is relatively small,
and has excellent machinability. In addition, no harmful substances are produced, which is good for workers and the environment.

COMPATIBILITY: Abrasive wheel is designed to fit most bench grinder machines.

 



Safety several work description
 

1. Select the grinding wheel with the spindle revolution of the grinder. Please check the grinding wheel before installation.
    Do not use it if any cracks or damages are found

2. The gap between inner hole of grinding wheel and spindle should not be too tight.
    After the shaft is bent, it should be stopped immediately and can continue to use after replacement of new parts

3. The shield used by the grinder should cover at least half of the diameter of the grinding wheel

4. After the grinder is started, it must be idled for at least 2 minutes. During idling, the operator should stand in a safe position,
   and it is strictly forbidden to stand in front of the grinding wheel or in the tangent direction

5. The rotation direction of the grinder should be correct, so that the grinding debris can only fly downward from the grinding wheel.
   Grinding is carried out after the grinder rotates smoothly. If the grinder runout is obvious, it should be stopped in time for dressing

6. The initial grinding can not be too hard, in order to avoid uneven grinding wheel force and accidents.
   Two people are not allowed to sharpen a knife on the same grinding wheel at the same time

7. It is strictly prohibited to use side grinding to avoid grinding wheel crushing.
   Do not grind copper, lead, wood and other things to prevent the grinding wheel from impingement

8. The sharpening time is long tool, should be timely cooling, to prevent hot hand

9. Regularly trim the surface balance of the grinding wheel. Keep it properly after use, do not knock against it, keep grinding wheel dry

10. always wear personal protective equipment, mask, eye mask, work cap, etc
