Technical Parameters

SALI Polifan Curve Radial Type Flap Disc Zinconia Oxide

4-1/2 inch 115*22.2mm
Curve Flap Disc Grain Grit ：#40 #60 #80 #120
Max：R.P.M 13300
 

【Unique Design】radial structural shape allows for precise grinding of fillet welds and repair applications.
The R angle of the 4-1/2-inch louver wheel can not only grind flat and curved surfaces, but also grind difficult-to-grind locations such as welds.
 

【Widely Used】power cutting and can be used for contour and edge work, its angled louver provides greater surface contact.
Mold repair of steel and non-ferrous metals, stainless steel plates, angle steel, stone and plastic, and grinding of welds, weld marks and burrs of stamping parts.
 

【High Cost-Effectiveness】The zirconia abrasive angle grinder sanding disc can continuously provide the sharpest cutting edge
providing the fastest cutting and the best utilization of the entire sand grain, thus achieving the longest service life.
 

【Efficient & Durable】 The sand distribution of the curved flap sanding discs is even and full, with stronger wear resistance and not easy to drop sand.
The Radial Shape grinding wheel has the characteristics of high elasticity, good self-sharpening, high grinding rate, good heat dissipation, low noise and long service life.
 

【Light and Durable】The fiberglass backing plate helps absorb vibrations, consumes during use, and won't get dirty
Lightweight, durable, and forms a strong bond with adhesives, won't contaminate the workpiece.
 

【Advantage】High stock removal rate ensures fast work progress and therefore significant wage cost savings.

Excellent tool life when working on fillet welds.

Precise and optimum grinding of the fillet weld geometry.

【Recommendations for use】After the circumference has worn down, can continue to be used as a conventional flap disc.

For fillet weld radii > 8 mm or weld seam thickness (throat thickness 'a') ≤ 6 mm with 90° joint, width at the radius: 16 mm

【Recommends 】Choice SALI Polifan Curve Flap Disc to achieve perfect results as quickly as possible.
【Recommends 】Choice SALI  Polifan Curve Flap Disc to sustainably reduce the vibration, noise and dust produced during use and to perceptibly improve comfort when working.
