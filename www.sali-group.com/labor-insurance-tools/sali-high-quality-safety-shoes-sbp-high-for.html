1. product Introduction

Labor protection shoes are a kind of shoes with safety protection effect on the foot. Our country carries on the classification of labor protection shoes according to the protective performance of shoes, there are mainly anti-static shoes and conductive shoes, insulating shoes, anti-hit shoes, anti-piercing safety shoes anti-acid and alkali shoes, anti-grease shoes, anti-slip shoes, anti-piercing shoes, anti-cold shoes, waterproof shoes and other special shoes.

SALI's labor protection shoes are high toughness insulation, anti-hit and anti-puncture labor protection shoes. SBP is the raw material to make the whole more comfortable, fit the foot and anti-wear feet. There are many sizes to choose from, exquisite style, middle and high top shoes can effectively protect the ankle, the thickened sole can effectively prevent puncture, safety and comfort are the industry leader
