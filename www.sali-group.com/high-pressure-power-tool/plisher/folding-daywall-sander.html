Description
Technical Parameters

Folding drywall sander, efficient sanding, flexible and portable, safe and easy to use.
Innovative folding structure, easy storage, adaptable to a variety of wall treatment needs, from old wall renovation to furniture details restoration, all in one machine!

【EFFICIENT DUST EXTRACTION SYSTEM】Our drywall sander is equipped with an advanced vacuum system featuring a ring cleaning brush, vacuum hose, and zippered vacuum bag, which effectively captures over 95% of dust, significantly reducing cleanup time.
 

【SURROUND LED LIGHT】Double-layered LED lights provide bright illumination for your workspace, making it easier to work at night or in dark environments. The soft, non-glaring light is gentle on your eyes, even during extended use.
 

【HIGH POWER PURE-COPPER MOTOR】Powered by a 1050W high-performance copper motor, this electric sander delivers strong power and high efficiency, making it durable and long-lasting. Ideal for sanding drywall, ceilings, floors, and more.
 

【6 VARIABLE SPEEDS】With a 5-speed variable control, you can easily adjust the speed from 1000 to 2100 RPM to achieve the ideal sanding effect based on your specific needs. Perfect for tasks like drywall grinding, ceiling sanding, floor residue clearing, paint removal, and loose plaster.
 

【FLEXIBLE HEAD】The sander's head rotates 360° for smooth, easy ceiling sanding, with a hook tension spring to keep it stable. If you have any questions about this wall sander, please feel free to contact us; we're here to help.
 

【FOLDABLE DESIGN】Our drywall sander features a foldable body to save storage space and comes with a convenient carrying bag for easy storage and portability. The dust collection hose can also connect to your own vacuum for enhanced dust management.
 

【VERSATILE SANDING OPTIONS】The 90° swiveling head rotates in any direction, staying flush with the wall for thorough sanding. Removable chassis edges allow easy access to corners or vertical walls, making it a great tool for both home DIYers and professionals.
