SALI ANGLE GRINDER MODEL 6100F 750W
 

Powerful Angle Grinder - With 750W max output power, this portable grinder can produce a no-load speed of 11000RPM for most demanding applications.
 

Ergonomic Design - Slim, lightweight, and compact body design can provide better comfort and reduce fatigue in professional cutting and grinding applications.
 

Convenient Operation - Integrated spindle lock on the disc grinder is for quicker and easier abrasive wheels replacement.
 

Side Handle - 2-Position auxiliary handle position for greater control, comfort & versatility in all applications.
 

Safety Protection & Durable - During operation, the protective cover of the portable grinder will prevent sparks and metal chips during use to protect you from injury.
