SALI's 2025 Review: A Year of Global Growth and Social Commitment

SALI Group, a leader in tool accessories, recently released its "2025 Year in Review," showcasing a year of remarkable strategic achievements across branding, operations, market expansion, and social responsibility.

Brand Influence Leaps to International Stage

The personal IP of SALI's chairman achieved milestone success, with the first theme video exceeding 4 million views, becoming a phenomenal communication case in the industry.

The company's brand image advertisement landed on the NASDAQ tower in New York's Times Square, receiving coverage from top global media such as the Associated Press, successfully promoting the Chinese brand to the world stage.

Intelligent Manufacturing Empowers High-Quality Development

In 2025, SALI completed the upgrade and transformation of 8 automated production lines and established a professional testing laboratory to provide comprehensive quality assurance for over 60 new products. The launch of the intelligent cloud warehouse system has significantly improved order processing efficiency, laying a solid foundation for global market supply.

Global Market Territory Continues to Expand

E-commerce business achieved historic breakthroughs, with 6 products entering the top three of the best-selling list and 14 products ranking among the top ten on Amazon's European and American sites. The number of customers in the European and American markets exceeded the 1 million mark, and brand influence continued to deepen. The company performed brilliantly at international exhibitions, with Mozambique's exhibition receiving a presidential visit, and the Sri Lankan and South African markets responding enthusiastically.

Social Responsibility Highlights Corporate Warmth

While achieving commercial success, SALI actively practices social responsibility. It launched a 10-day food and beverage donation campaign in Mozambique, benefiting thousands of families; and donated necessities and toys to an orphanage in Côte d'Ivoire, conveying corporate warmth and humanistic care.

Industry Benchmark Position Solidified

With 22 years of industry experience, an export network covering more than 160 countries and a modern 30,000-square-meter factory area, SALI has become a leading enterprise in the industry with both scale and professionalism. The brilliant achievements in 2025 mark a solid step forward for the company on the path of "building a better future with global users".
