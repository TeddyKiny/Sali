How to maintain
 

【Keep dry】 After using the garden art cutting, it should be cleaned immediately and keeps dry to prevent rust.
 

【Oil】 Apply lubricating oil at each connection to maintain the flexibility of gardening, prevent parts from rusting parts, and then affect the service life.
 

【Regular blade grinding】Gardening is often used to trim trees, shrubs and lawns, which is easy to damage the cutting blade. Regular cutting of cutting blade and grinding can ensure the shear effect of garden cutting.
 

【Do not use gardening to cut hard items】 Horticultural cutting is mainly used for pruning plants, and should not be used for hard items, such as metal wires, iron wires, etc., so as not to damage the cut blade.
 

【Pay attention when storing】 Long -term unused garden cuts should be stored in places where moisture -proof, ventilation, and drying to prevent rust.
 

【Pay attention to usage】In the process of using garden cutting, be careful not to cut too large branches or schools, so as to avoid too large shear power and damage garden cutting.
When cutting the branches, choose the branches that are trimmed. The incision should be tough and cannot have burrs.
