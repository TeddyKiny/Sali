One Machine, All-in-One Cleaning Power

Tired of bulky, hard-to-use traditional car washers with low efficiency?
This high-performance cordless pressure
washer redefines "effortless cleaning" with cutting-edge tech-whether tackling stubborn grime or giving your vehicle a showroom shine, from your driveway to outdoor adventures, it's your ultimate all-in-one solution.

1. Powerful Core: Brushless Motor + High Pressure for Ultimate Cleaning
Equipped with an "energy-efficient brushless motor", it delivers stable, uninterrupted power, generating "1.5MPa high-pressure water jets" (equivalent to 15 kg/cm² pressure) to blast away mud, sand, and grease from your car's surface-up to 30% faster than standard washers.
With a "3.5L/min high-flow water intake", it continuously draws and sprays water without interruption, effortlessly stripping away thick mud, bug splatters, or oil stains.
2. Foam Boost: Dense Foam Cannon for Stubborn Stains
Comes with a "professional foam cannon nozzle" that mixes car shampoo seamlessly with high-pressure water, producing "thick clinging foam" that blankets your car's surface.
The rich foam softens stubborn contaminants like tree sap, bird droppings, or road tar, making them slide off with just a light rinse or sponge wipe-no harsh scrubbing needed, and zero risk of damaging your paint. Achieve that "just-washed" showroom finish, even for beginners.
3. Flexible Cleaning: 6-in-1 Rotary Nozzle for Every Need
The " 6-in-1 rotary spray nozzle " lets you switch between multiple cleaning modes "with a single twist"-no need to swap parts.
Choose from "powerful jet/flat fan spray/mist mode/foam mode (compatible)/fine stream/intense impact" (modes may vary by model) to match every cleaning task.
Use the powerful jet for tires and wheels;
The flat fan for large surface areas;
The fine stream for crevices like side mirrors or door handles. Whether it's a quick rinse or a full-detail clean, this machine handles it all with precision.

4. Lightweight & User-Friendly: Gentle on Hands, Easy for All

Say goodbye to heavy, cumbersome washers. The lightweight, breathable-material body is designed to be easy to lift and maneuver-even for extended use. The ergonomic handle features anti-slip textures, ensuring a secure grip for users of all strengths, including seniors or women. Compact and easy to handle, it's perfect for anyone who wants a stress-free washing experience.
5. Complete Kit: Durable Storage Case for On-the-Go Convenience
Comes with a "heavy-duty engineering case" that protects the washer, hose, nozzles, and power cord (all accessories included), keeping everything organized and preventing loss.
The rugged case is built to withstand drops and bumps, making it ideal for both garage storage and outdoor use. Some models even feature a "handle or wheels" for effortless transport-whether you're cleaning your car at home, washing camping gear, or tackling outdoor equipment, it's ready to go whenever you are.
