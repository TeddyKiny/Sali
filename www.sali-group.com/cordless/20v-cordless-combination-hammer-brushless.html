  • Durable and reliable: The brushless motor reduces the wear of the carbon brush, reduces the failure rate, and extends the service life of the electric hammer.

    Common questions and answers

  • Question: What are the advantages of brushless motors compared with brushed motors?

    Answer: Brushless motors have no carbon brush friction, are quieter during operation, have more stable power output, higher efficiency, and longer service life, reducing later maintenance costs and the trouble of replacing carbon brushes.


  • Question: If the battery is exhausted during the use of the electric hammer, will it damage the battery?

    Answer: No, our battery pack has a built-in over-discharge protection circuit. When the power is too low, it will automatically cut off the power supply to avoid damage to the battery caused by excessive discharge, effectively extending the battery life.


  • Question: Is this electric hammer suitable for use in a humid environment?

    Answer: The electric hammer has a certain splashproof design, but it is not recommended to use it in a humid or water-stained environment for a long time. If you work in a relatively humid environment, please wipe the body dry in time to ensure the performance and service life of the electric hammer.


  • Question: What is the warranty policy of the electric hammer?

    Answer: We provide a one-year warranty service. During the warranty period, if the product has non-human quality problems, we will repair or replace parts for you free of charge.


  • Question: How long does it take to fully charge two battery packs?

    Answer: Using our standard fast charger, it takes about 3-5 hours to fully charge. The specific charging time may vary slightly due to factors such as the remaining battery power and ambient temperature.


  • Question: If I need to purchase an additional battery pack, where can I buy it?

    Answer: You can purchase an adapted battery pack through our official website, authorized e-commerce platform or local dealer. Please look for our brand and model when purchasing to ensure the compatibility and performance of the battery.
