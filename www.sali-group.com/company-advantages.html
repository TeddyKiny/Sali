OUR STRENGTHS
 
01/

SALI boasts a diverse product lineup with over 3000 SKUs, catering to both DIY and professional sectors. It includes powertool accessories, power tools, hand tools, pneumatic tools, and covers various applications such as metalworking, stone processing, woodworking, floor and wall tiling, gardening, and construction. The products address common needs like cutting, sanding, polishing, drilling, fastening, measuring, lifting, welding, and disassembly.

02/

SALI has a high-standard supplier screening mechanism. Over 20 years since its establishment, the company has developed a comprehensive system for supplier selection criteria and evaluation, effectively preventing quality issues from the source.

03/

SALI has a professional inspection and testing mechanism. The company conducts quality spot checks on each batch of outgoing products to prevent substandard items from entering the market. Additionally, the company has a specialized evaluation laboratory where only products that pass the evaluation are allowed for mass production, ensuring that every product released by the company withstands the test of time. Our company supports remote access to the camera inside the testing room for customers, allowing them to stay updated in real-time on product quality evaluation results. Furthermore, we can conduct special project tests based on customer requirements.

04/

We have established stock preparation mechanisms for best-selling products. With over 8,000 square meters of warehouse space capable of holding 12,000 cubic meters of goods, we maintain regular stock levels for popular items to ensure same-day order fulfillment and shipping.

05/

SALI enables efficient logistics. We collaborate with renowned international logistics companies, providing special declaration channels for products with batteries, achieving a compliance clearance rate of 99.6%. Through rational allocation, the average time for goods to reach port has been decreasing annually, while reducing transfer steps significantly lowers the cargo damage rate.

06/

SALI boasts a professional sales service team, with 70% of its members having over 8 years of experience. We have established specialized service groups, and our multilingual account managers can communicate seamlessly with clients worldwide. Additionally, we create 'Tool and accessories of power toolss Lifecycle Archives' for each key client and set up VIP customer emergency mechanisms to ensure rapid response and resource allocation.

07/

SALI's products are widely welcomed by global customers. Our products are not only favored by major member countries of the Belt and Road Initiative but also shine brightly in developed Western and European countries. Some of our powertool accessories consistently rank among the top three bestsellers on Amazon.
