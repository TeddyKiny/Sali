What is PU FOAM?

PU FOAM is a product that combines aerosol and polyurethane foam technologies. It is a special polyurethane product composed of polyurethane prepolymers, foaming agents, catalysts, and other components. This unique combination gives it excellent performance characteristics, making it widely used in various industries.

Excellent Properties of PU FOAM

(1) Fast Curing for Efficient Construction

One of the significant advantages of PU FOAM is its fast curing time. It has a surface drying time of only 10 minutes, which allows for quick progress in construction. The full drying time varies with temperature and humidity. In summer, it can fully dry within 4-6 hours, while in winter, it may take 24 hours or even longer. This fast curing feature greatly improves construction efficiency, reducing the overall construction period and enabling projects to be completed on schedule.

(2) Powerful Functional Characteristics

After curing, the foam of PU FOAM has multiple effects such as filling, bonding, sealing, heat insulation, and sound absorption. In the construction field, it can be used for filling gaps in buildings, waterproofing and plugging leaks, and heat preservation. In industrial production, it plays a role in sealing, shock absorption, and sound insulation for electronic equipment, automobile manufacturing, aerospace, and other industries. For daily maintenance and DIY enthusiasts, it is a practical tool for repairing holes and gaps in homes and realizing creative productions. Its versatility makes it applicable to a wide range of scenarios.

(3) Excellent Weather Resistance and Stability

PU FOAM can maintain its elasticity and bonding strength within a temperature range of -10℃ to 80℃, ensuring stable performance in different climatic conditions. Under normal usage conditions, its service life is not less than ten years, which reflects its reliable stability. This long - term stability ensures that it can play a good role in various applications for a long time, reducing the need for frequent maintenance and replacement.

Wide Application Fields of PU FOAM

(1) Construction Field

In the construction industry, PU FOAM is of great importance. During door and window installation, it is used for gap filling, sealing, and fixed bonding, ensuring the tightness and stability of doors and windows. It is also widely used in building gap filling, waterproofing and leakage plugging, and heat insulation, contributing to improving the quality and energy efficiency of buildings.

(2) Industrial Manufacturing

In industrial manufacturing, PU FOAM plays a key role. In electronic equipment, it is used for sealing to prevent dust and moisture from entering, ensuring the normal operation of equipment. In automobile manufacturing, it is applied for sealing and shock absorption, improving the performance and safety of automobiles. In the aerospace field, it also serves important functions in sealing and insulation.

(3) Daily Maintenance and DIY

For daily home maintenance, PU FOAM is very useful for repairing holes, gaps, and other defects, making the home environment more perfect. DIY enthusiasts can use it in creative productions to realize their design ideas and save costs. Its practicality in daily life makes it popular among ordinary users.

How to Correctly Use PU FOAM?

(1) Preparation Before Use

Before construction, the construction surface must be treated. It is necessary to remove oil stains and floating dust on the surface and spray water to moisten it, which helps to improve the bonding effect of the foam. At the same time, the tank must be fully shaken to ensure that the materials inside are evenly mixed, so as to ensure the performance of the foam during use.

(2) Construction Operation Steps

For gun - type and tube - type PU FOAM, the usage methods are slightly different. For gun - type PU FOAM, first connect the spray gun correctly, then adjust the flow according to the needs. When spraying, hold the gun at a certain angle and spray evenly. Control the filling amount to avoid excessive or insufficient filling. For tube - type PU FOAM, directly install the tube on the tank, and then spray according to the same method as the gun - type, paying attention to the control of the filling amount.

(3) Post - Construction Treatment

After the foam is cured, it needs to be processed. Use a knife to cut off the excess foam and then polish the surface to make it flat. After that, apply protective layers such as cement mortar and paint on the surface to enhance the durability and appearance of the foam. These post - construction treatment steps are essential to ensure the final effect of the project.
