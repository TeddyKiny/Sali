SALI grinding wheel product classification

Due to the large number of products of the company, we have made a rough classification of its type, performance and cut objects.

1. Classified by product type:

(1) Conventional: Blue trademark, black sand for grinding disc products.

(2) Sharp type: blue trademark, the grinding disc product is red sand, and it is also suitable for stainless steel workpieces.

(3) Durable type or special grade: red trademark, the grinding disc product is red sand, and at the same time, half a mesh is added to the outside

2. Classified by product performance:

(1) Conventional type

This type of product is economical and suitable for a large customer base in the market. The types and specifications of cutting and grinding workpieces are complex and can meet the needs of most customers.

(2) Sharp type

Sharp products have high cutting and grinding efficiency, and the abrasive grains have good self-sharpening properties during use. Under the action of grinding force, new cutting edges can be quickly exposed, so the chip evacuation is smooth, the heat dissipation is good, and the workpiece is not burned. The hand feels good at the time and the labor intensity is low. It is suitable for cutting various types of materials and round steel with large cross-sectional area and high hardness. It is mostly used in shipyards, casting factories, mold processing plants, etc.

(3) Premium type

Under the premise of ensuring that the product does not burn the workpiece, the special-grade product enables the cutting effect of the abrasive particles to be fully utilized, the cutting and grinding ratio is high, and the product service life is long; at the same time, the operator does not need to frequently replace the product used, avoiding multiple disassembly Product is a waste of time and high efficiency. It is suitable for the grinding of workpieces with high strength requirements, high speed of grinding tools, and high durability requirements. It is mostly used in shipyards, casting factories, etc.

3. Classification according to cutting and grinding objects:

(1) Conventional type

The product is a blue trademark and black sand. It is mainly used in the cutting and grinding of ordinary steel. It has a wide range of applications and can meet the needs of different customers.

(2) Cutting and grinding stainless steel type

The product is a blue trademark and red sand. It is specially designed for the characteristics of high hardness of stainless steel and easy burns of the workpiece during cutting. The product is specially designed with good sharpness, good heat dissipation performance during cutting, no burns to the workpiece, and high precision of the machined surface.

The above is our rough classification of products. If you need to know more about it, you can get the product catalog from us through our official website or via email.
