1. product Introduction

A gasoline drill, also known as a portable gasoline powered drill, is a tool used to directly mine rock. It drilled holes in rock formations for explosives to blast through the rock to complete quarrying or other masonry work. In addition, it can also be adapted as a destroyer for breaking hard layers such as concrete.

Portable power drill is by a petrol engine, the impact of impact drill rod piston, using the rotation of the ratchet wheel to make drill rotating impact on the rock, rock hit the up and down movement of the piston, discharge gas into the heart of the drill hole,According to the different hardness of rock, with different hardness of alloy brazing head. According to the size of the hole, the replacement of different sizes of alloy bit, suitable for all kinds of mine drilling, blasting.
