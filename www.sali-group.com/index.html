About
our company

Zhejiang Sali Abrasive Technology Co., Ltd. is an integrated company specializing in research and development, manufacturing, global trade, and brand operation. With over two decades of expertise in professional power tools,accessories, and hand tools. Its self-owned brand SALI has secured trademark registrations in over 150 countries since its launch in 2010. The company has established a three-dimensional marketing system spanning across five continents, with products exported to more than 160 countries and regions. As of March 2025, it has signed long-term strategic cooperation agreements with agents from 45 countries. The company holds dual ISO 9001/14001 certifications and has implemented a comprehensive quality traceability system. Its products have obtained multiple international authoritative certifications, including MPA, EN12413, and EU safety standards.
