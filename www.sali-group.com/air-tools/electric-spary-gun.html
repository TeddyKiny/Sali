SALI ELECTRIC SPRAY GUN



3 different Spray Patterns (Drehrn/Vertical/Horizontal) are adjusted easily by simply rotating the air cap to select the pattern that best suits your application


With a 550W power cord makes it simple and convenient, allowing it to move more freely and change the working distance according to the actual situation


High capacity with 800ML containers, you don't need to add the diluted paint often to save painting time


Detachable design allows all parts of the spray gun can be separated and cleaned easily after finishing


Flow control knob helps to control the spray flow for the coating accordingly


Choose 1 nozzles-1.8MM that are more suitable for different jobs



Warm Tips:

Before using a paint gun, you should choose the proper copper nozzle and spray pattern. Check out if the paint gun container and the L-type suction pipe are tightened, besides, we recommend you test on cardboard or wood before spraying important objects such as walls or furniture.


Before diluting, please remove the dirt from the paint.


Please add water (or paint thinner) to dilute the paint and stir to ensure that the paint passes through the paint gun viscosity cup smoothly within 45 seconds.


When you use a paint gun for the first time, you need to press the trigger and wait for 20 seconds (the purpose is to suck enough air into the container and balance the air pressure). The sprayer can spray smoothly doing so.


While spraying, pay attention to the sprayer. If it got hot, stop and wait for several seconds.


After spraying, add some paint thinner into the container, screw it and spray the thinner out (the purpose is to clean the sprayer thoroughly).


Finally, disassemble the paint gun and clean each part.


Last but not least, please remember to clean the Spray Paint Gun with the cleaning brush after every use! Each part of the sprayer is detachable.
