1. product Introduction

Automobile tire charging pump, vehicle pump is also called inflator, inflator, vehicle charging pump, through the operation of the internal motor to work. When pumping, the valve of the connecting device is opened by the atmospheric pressure, and the gas enters the cylinder, and when the tire is pumped, the valve is closed by the air pressure inside the cylinder, and the gas enters the tire. It is also the principle of using atmospheric pressure to inflate with cars, balls and rubber boats.

4. Application

 

1. Plug in the air charging pump power supply on the cigarette lighter.

2. Place the air pump next to the tire that needs to be inflated.

3. Connect the air charging nozzle.

4, through the button to select the pressure to inflate.

5. Press the on key at the bottom to start inflating automatically.
