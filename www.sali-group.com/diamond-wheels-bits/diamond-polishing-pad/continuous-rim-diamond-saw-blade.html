SALI brand 7 inch (180mm) segment turbo diamond saw blade’s body is made by high-strength 65Mn steel and the segment is pressed by diamond and metal powder under high temperature and high pressure, which has extended performance diamond matrix provides long life and enhanced material cutting. General-purpose style can use for cutting all types of tile and stone. We can supply copper plating washer to change the hole size from big to small. 12mm segment height, wider than other normal products, will have longer lifetime. And it can use for dry and wet cutting, ultra sharp, the edges do not protrude after cutting. If used after adding water, the service life will be longer. About the size, except 180mm, we can also supply 105mm, 115mm, 125mm, 230mm, 300mm, 350mm these sizes if you need.
