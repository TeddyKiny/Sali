SALI Brushless Cordless Angle Grinder 20V 4.0Ah*2

Rechargeable multifunctional electric tool for polishing, cutting, electric grinding and polishing. 

4inch/100mm 4.5Inch/115mm size for your choice.


Super power combination: dual 20V 4.0Ah battery packs provide surging power, and with advanced brushless motors, powerful torque can be instantly released

Easily cope with various high-intensity grinding and cutting tasks, even hard alloy steel can be efficiently processed.


Super long battery life guarantee: two large-capacity batteries are used alternately, and the battery life is greatly extended.

A full charge can meet the regular workload of a whole day, without frequent interruptions to find power, which greatly improves the continuity of work.


Precise and efficient control: The precise control characteristics of the brushless motor make the angle grinder responsive during operation, and can accurately control the grinding force and angle to achieve fine processing. It is especially suitable for jewelry processing, handicraft production and other fields with high precision requirements.


Durable and reliable choice: The body made of high-quality materials is sturdy and durable, and can withstand the test of harsh working environments.

Precise control experience: The body is ergonomically designed, comfortable to hold, and equipped with a precise speed adjustment knob. Users can easily adjust to the appropriate speed according to different cutting materials and operation requirements, achieving precise and efficient operation.
