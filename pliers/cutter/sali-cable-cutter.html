Technical Parameters



Feature:

[High Hard Material] Forged with chrome vanadium steel through heat treatment that makes a long lifespan.Through high-frequency quenching, polished and hardened, the blades of cable cutting pliers has strong cutting force.High hardness, durability and wear-resistance.

[Sharp and Smooth Cuts] Designed precision-machined blades and shear-type jaws, our cable cutter pliers can make exact and clean cuts without fraying or smashing the cable end. 

[High-Leverage]The special high-leverage design makes the product have super long handle; Compared with other ordinary tools on the market, this labor-saving design conforms to ergonomics, helps to save most of the effort, effectively prevents hand fatigue, and makes the work become easier

[Non-slip Handle]The cable cutters heavy duty ensure a better and more stable grip; The handle is covered with a flexible and non-slip double color dipped plastic to increase friction and provide a more comfortable grip.

[One-hand Operation] Accurate cutting, easy to use even with one hand, it is very suitable for working in narrow confined space; The handle is comfortable and easy to spot in your tool bag.

[ Widely Used ] This cable cutter has a wide range of applications, which can cuts for aluminum, copper, communications cable etc.; PLEASE NOTE: The dipped plastic handle is not insulated, please do not use it to cut the live wires.
