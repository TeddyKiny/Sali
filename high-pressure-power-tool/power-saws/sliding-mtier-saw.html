Products Precautions

  • 1 The operator must be qualified after training. Non -machine operators must not operate the machine.

  • 2 The operator is wearing a work clothes and a dust -proof mask. It is strictly forbidden to wear gloves. The spirit must be highly concentrated during work. For those who stay long, the hair must be raised. 

  • 3 Check before operation. The saw blade must be flat, smooth, rustless, and sharp, and there is a proper sawing path. There must be no continuous lack of teeth. If there is a crack, the length of the saw blade shall not exceed the end of the 20mm crack to stop the cracks to prevent continuing expansion.

  • 3 Test the machine before processing to check whether the machine is running normally. Pay special attention to whether the saw blade turns correctly.

  • 4 When tailoring, press the wood tightly in the left hand, the wood should be close to the baffle, and the right hand is tapped.

  • 5 Senging persons from sawnooth must not be less than 300mq: Attention should be concentrated.

  • 6 In the work, if a power outage is encountered, the switch should be turned off to disconnect the power to prevent the mechanical operating by itself.

  • 7 The machine and equipment are abnormal or an unexpected accident should be cleared and checked immediately.
