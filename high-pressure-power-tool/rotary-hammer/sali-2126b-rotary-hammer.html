Care and maintenance

1. Cleaning after use:

After each use of the electric hammer, the dust and debris on its surface should be cleaned in time. You can use a clean damp cloth to wipe the outer shell, pay special attention to the drill clamp, vents and other parts to prevent dust accumulation from affecting heat dissipation and performance.

2. Check and tighten the screws:

Regularly check whether the screws on the electric hammer are loose, including the drill clamp screws, handle screws, etc. If they are loose, they should be tightened with appropriate tools.

For example, loose drill clamp screws may cause the drill bit to shake during work, which not only affects the drilling accuracy, but also may damage the electric hammer.

3. Lubrication parts:

Properly lubricate the moving parts of the electric hammer, such as gears, connecting rods, etc. But be careful to use appropriate lubricants, and do not use too much to avoid oil accumulation.

4. Storage environment:

Store the electric hammer in a dry, well-ventilated place to avoid humid and high temperature environments. At the same time, prevent heavy objects from squeezing and colliding.

5. Check the wires and plugs regularly:

Check whether the wires are damaged or aging, and whether the plugs are in good contact. If there are any problems, they should be replaced in time.
