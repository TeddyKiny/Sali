When cleaning stainless steel, it is advisible to use a stainless steel bristle wire brush.

-A plain carbon steel brush can contaminate the stainless steel and cause rust spots to appear.

-When cleaning the soft surfaces or in potentially flammable environments where non-sparking tools are required, it is advisible to use a brass bristle wire brush.
