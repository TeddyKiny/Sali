Garden Shear Tools

Jun 09, 2022

Production Introduction

Garden Shears and Hand Pruner：





A must-have pruning tool for every gardener and flower lover.

Ordinary Garden Shears and Hand Pruner generally cut branches below 3 cm, as long as they can be included in the cutting opening, they can be cut off.

During operation, if you hold the scissors with your right hand, use your left hand to push the thick branches in the direction of the small pieces of the scissors, and you will be able to solve it.


Heavy Duty Steel Handle Lopper:



For pruning and cutting of small tree trunks, long handle makes it easy to prune small tree trunks


Hedge shears:


The bar blade of the hedge shear is very long, and it can cut off a piece of treetop with a tie cut, and it can trim the top and side of the hedge very smoothly. It is one of the most commonly used tools for pruning plants.

However, the blade surface of the hedge shears is relatively thin, and can only be used to cut young shoots flat. It cannot prune thick branches that are fully lignified. If individual thick branches emerge from hedgerows, they should be cut off with ordinary pruning shears first, and then Use hedge shears again.


Telescoping Aluminum Handle Lopper or Shears:


When the tree is too high, it is inconvenient to prune branches on the ladder, so it is necessary to use telescopic scissors with aluminum handles

In fact, it is an extension of the human arm. It is equipped with a telescopic aluminum alloy handle, which can be adjusted with the height of the pruning.

completed the work efficiently


Daily maintenance:

1. After working every day, the used tools should be sorted out, soil and sundries should be removed, wiped dry, and placed in a ventilated place to keep them dry to avoid rusting.

2. Protecting the cutting edge: Special attention should be paid to the protection of the part with the cutting edge in the gardening tool. It should be fully immersed in oil during storage. It is best to wrap it with wax paper to avoid tilting and overlapping, preventing pressure, bending and deformation. Part of the sharpening should have special tools to ensure the sharpening angle and prolong the service life.

3. Keep it properly. After the operation is over, when it has been idle for a long time, the tray should be properly stored, cleaned, wiped dry, coated with anti-rust oil on the metal surface, and placed in an appropriate place. Layer squeeze; keep in a ventilated dry place.

4. Pay attention to rust prevention. The working parts of gardening tools are mostly made of metal materials, and metal materials are easy to rust. Once rusted, it will affect the use, and may lose its use value, and it is not easy to remove after rusting, so special attention should be paid during use. Anti-rust treatment.
