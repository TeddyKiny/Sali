SALI Was Covered By AP And Other International Media Outlets.

Nov 15, 2025

Leave a message

AP and other major international media outlets covered SALI.

 

 

The following is the original text from The Associated Press：

 

SALI Celebrates Milestone of One Million Global Users, Strengthens Position in European and American Markets!

 

Published

 

Nov 10, 2025 5:27am EST

 

Jinhua, Zhejiang--(Newsfile Corp. - November 10, 2025) - SALI, a leading global manufacturer of power tool accessories, today announced that it has officially surpassed one million users across Europe and the United States, marking a significant milestone in the brand's international growth journey.

 

 

SALI Power Tool Accessories - Bestsellers on Amazon Europe & U.S.

 

This achievement underscores SALI's increasing recognition among professionals and consumers for its commitment to safety, quality, and performance.

 

One Million Users Milestone Driven by Amazon Success

 

In 2025, SALI achieved record-breaking sales performance across Amazon's U.S. and European platforms. Six core products-including 4.5-inch grinding discs, 4.5-inch metal grinding wheels, and steel drill bits-secured positions in the Amazon Best Seller Top 3, while 14 additional products ranked in the Top 10 in their respective categories. Through its e-commerce channels alone, SALI now serves over 500,000 online users, complemented by its offline distribution network reaching a total of over one million customers across Western markets.

 

Commitment to Safety and Quality

 

SALI's success is driven by its long-standing commitment to safety and craftsmanship. Founder and Chairman Peter Hu, who began his career as a craftsman at the age of 16, emphasizes:

 

"Should any personal injury occur due to SALI product defects, SALI assumes full responsibility."

 

This pledge reflects SALI's deep respect for professional tool users and its philosophy of "Safety & Efficiency" as the cornerstone of its R&D and manufacturing systems.

 

SALI Chairman Peter Hu Appearing with Clients (Mr. Peter Hu is the third from the right)

 

To meet the stringent standards of Western markets, SALI has developed a comprehensive quality assurance chain:

 

  • Rigorous supplier screening to ensure raw material excellence.
  • In-house testing labs and batch inspections to eliminate defects.
  • Transparent quality monitoring, allowing customers to remotely observe testing procedures.

 

Enhanced Logistics and Customer Experience

 

SALI continues to refine its global logistics and service capabilities:

 

Partnerships with global logistics leaders ensure smooth handling of battery-equipped tools, achieving a 99.6% customs clearance rate and faster deliveries.

 

A multilingual sales and service team (70% with 8+ years of experience) offers seamless communication and customized "Tool Consumables Lifecycle Profiles" for clients worldwide.

 

Looking Ahead

 

Building on this momentum, SALI will accelerate its global expansion strategy, introducing new product lines and strengthening cooperation with local distributors in Europe, North America, and emerging markets. The company aims to consolidate its leadership position in the power tool consumables sector through technological innovation and customer-centric service.

 

ALI Tools Global Strategic Layout

 

About SALI

 

Founded in 2010, Zhejiang Sali Abrasive Technology Co., Ltd. is a comprehensive manufacturer specializing in power tool accessories, hand tools, and related consumables. The company has registered its "SALI" trademark in over 190 countries and regions, and its products are sold in more than 160 markets globally.

 

Serving professionals in metalworking, woodworking, stone processing, construction, landscaping, and automotive repair, SALI continues to deliver high-quality, safe, and reliable tools that help users work smarter and more efficiently.
