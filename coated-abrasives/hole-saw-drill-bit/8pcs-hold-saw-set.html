Technical Parameters


SALI Brand Hold Saw Set-8pcs set

[Professional Material] Made of carbon steel,which has the advantages of oil resistance, rust resistance, durability. Ultra sharp serrations for faster and cleaner cutting;

[Wide Application] Ultra sharp serrations for faster and cleaner cutting, which is ideal for softwood, plywood, plastic plate, plasterboard and thin PVC board;

[Easy to Install] Quickly and easily replace all tools needed for the saw. The set included hex wrench and mounting plate for tool installation;

[Portable Storage Case] Cutting tools are neatly stored in a customized case for easy carry and organization. Compact slots hold each component in place for increased efficiency.






NOTE:


1. Please wear protective glasses and gloves when cutting, because it is safe for you.


2. Put all accessories in the correct position, the mounting plate should be located between the blade and the nut to prevent the blade from being damaged. A drill must be installed to drill a safety hole to make the saw work easier.


3. Do not over-tighten grub screws, remove debris from teeth for cutting efficiency.


4. After using a small amount of anti-rust oil on the surface of the filter, it can avoid natural oxidation and can increase its service life.
